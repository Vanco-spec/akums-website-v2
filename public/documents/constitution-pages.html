AKUMS Constitution (Revised 2026 ) PREAMBLE
We, the MBChB students of Kenyatta University ,
ACKNOW LEDGING the guidance and providence of Almighty God in all endeavours ;
RECOGNIZING the necessity of a unified student body to represent, protect, and
advance the interests of medical students at Kenyatta University;
COMMITTING to uphold professionalism, in tegrity, and ethical responsibility in all
aspects of medi cal training and practice;
VALO RIZING collaboration with local and international institutions to enrich learning
and service opportunities;
UNDERSTANDING the importance of solidarity, mutual support , and active
participation in fostering a vibrant medical student community;
Do hereby establish the Association of Kenyatta University Medical Students (AKUMS) ,
adopt this Constitution as the supreme guiding document of our Association, and pledge to
upho ld its principles and objectives for the benefit of our members, the medical profession,
and society at large.
MAY GOD BLESS AKUMS

AKUMS Constitution (Revised 2026 ) CHAPTER ONE: NAME AND PURPOSE OF THE ASSOCIATION
Article 1: Name
1. The Association shall be known as the Associat ion of Kenyatta University Medical
Students (AKUMS) , hereinafter referred to as “the Association.”
2. The official abbreviation of the Association shall be AKUMS .
3. No other name, acronym, or variation thereof shall be used in any
advertisement, correspondence, publication, event, or representation of the
Association without the express written approval of the Executive Council.
Article 2: Scope of operation
1. The Association shall operate within the jurisdiction of Kenyatta University ,
including all its constitue nt campuses offering medical training under the School of
Medicine.
2. The Association may, upon approval by the Executive Council and the Dean of the
School of Medicine, collaborate with recognized national and international bodies
with similar aims and obje ctives.
3. All activities of the Association shall be conducted in accordance with the Kenyatta
University Rules and Regulations and other relevant statutes.
Article 3: Aims and objectives
The aims and objectives of the Association shall be to:
1. Promote academ ic excellence, research competence, and professional ethics among
medical students of Kenyatta University.
2. Empower medical students to utilize their knowledge, skills, and capacities for the
benefit of the community and the nation at large.
3. Provide a forum for medical students to discuss matters related to health, education,
science, and social welfare, and to formulate policies that enhance academic and
professional growth.
4. Serve as the official represe
ntative body of all medical students in matters affect ing
their academic, social, and welfare interests before the University Administration and
external institutions.
5. Establish and maintain collaboration with other medical student associations,
professional bodies, and international organizations in furthera nce of the
Association’s objectives.
6. Promote mentorship, leadership development, and character building to nurture
holistic, competent, and compassionate future healthcare professionals.
7. Foster unity, cooperation, and a spirit of professionalism among memb ers through
teamwork, discipline, and mutual respect.
8. Engage in community outreach, public health education, and social responsibility
programs including but not limited to drug awareness, HIV/AIDS prevention, sexual
and reproductive health, nutrition, and charity initiatives.
9. Collaborate with governmental and non -governmental organizations in activities
aimed at improving community health and welfare.
10. Promote physical, mental, and emotional well -being of members through advocacy,
counselling, and support p rograms.

AKUMS Constitution (Revised 2026 ) CHAPTER TWO: ME MBERSHIP
Article 4 : Membership
1. Membership of the Association shall comprise:
a) Ordinary Members – All students duly enrolled at Kenyatta University pursuing
the Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBChB) degree shall be
members of the Associatio n by default.
b) Alumni Members – Any alumnus of Kenyatta University who pursued the
Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery degree may, upon registration and
payment of the prescribed fee, be recognized as a member of the Association.
c) Affiliate Me mbers – Persons, institutions, or organizations whose objectives align
with those of the Association may, upon approval by the Executive Council, be
admitted as affiliate members without voting rights.
2. Admission to membership shall be open without discrimi nation on the basis of
gender, race, religion, or political affiliation.
3. Every member shall be entered into the Association’s register maintained by the
Office of the Secretary General.
Article 5 : Enrolment and subscription fees
1. Registration shall be condu cted once upon admissio n to the Association at an
enrolment fee of Kenya Shillings One Hundred ( Ksh. 100/=) .
2. Semester subscriptions shall be payable at the beginning of every semester at a fee of
Kenya Shillings One Hundred ( Ksh. 100/=) or as may be revise d from time to time
by the Executive Council with the approval of the General Assembly.
3. Non-payment of subscription fees for two consecutive semesters without valid reason
shall render one an inactive member until arrears are settled.
4. The Treasurer shall m aintain accurate financial records of all registration a
nd
subscription payments.
Article 6 : Rights and privileges of members
1. Every member shall have fair and equitable access to all services, facilities, and
programs of the Association through lawful mean s.
2. Only registered and fully subscribed ordinary members (in accordance with article 4
)shall :
a) Be eligible to vote in any election or decision of the Association.
b) Be eligible to contest for any elective position within the Association.
3. Every member s hall have the right to:
a) Participate in meetings, discussions, and decision -making processes of the
Association.
b) Question the credibility or performance of any office bearer or organ of the
Association in accordance with this Constitution.
c) Cast a vote of no confidence in a non -performing office bearer through a
democratic process as prescribed in this Constitution.
4. All decisions of the Association shall be made through an open, democratic, and
participatory process , unless otherwise provided herein.

AKUMS Constitution (Revised 2026 ) Article 7 : Cessation of membership
Membership shall cease under the following circumstances:
1. Upon completion of the Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery program at
Kenyatta University, unless the member registers as an alumnus.
2. Upon failur e to pa y prescribed enrolment fee
3. Upon voluntary resignation submitted in writing to the Secretary General and
approved by the Executive Council.
4. Upon disciplinary expulsion in accordance with Article 5 of this Chapter.
5. Upon death of the member.
6. Upon discontinuat ion, transfer, or expulsion from the University.
A member who ceases to belong to the Association shall forfeit all rights and privileges
thereof.
Article 8 : Discipline
1. Discipline shall mean compliance with proper conduct as embodied in this
Constitution a nd all rules or regulations enacted by the organs of the Association.
2. Any member who deliberately undermines, disrupts, or brings disrepute to the
Association, its activities, or its name shall be subject to disciplinary action.
3. Disciplinary measures may i nclude a warning, suspension, or excommunication
(expulsion), depending on the gravity of the offence.
4. Before any penalty is imposed, the accused member shall be given a fair opportunity
to defend themselves before the Disciplinary Committee , whose recomme ndations
shall be ratified by a simple majority of the Executive Council or the General
Assembly as appropriate.
5. Any expelled member may appeal the decision in writing to the General Assembly
within fourteen (14) days from the date of communication of the disciplinary
decision.

AKUMS Constitution (Revised 2026 ) CHAPTER THREE: ORGANS OF THE ASSOCIATION
Article 9 : Organs of the Association
1. The governing organs of the Association shall be:
a) The Annual General Meeting (AGM) ;
b) The General Assembly ;
c) The Executive Council ;
d) Stand ing and Ad hoc Committees as established under this Constitution.
2. These organs shall derive their authority from this Constitution and shall be bound by
its provisions in the execution of their respective mandates.
3. The powers and functions of each organ sh all be exercised in accordance with the
principles of transparency, accountability, inclusivity, and democratic participation.
Article 10: Annual General Meeting (AGM)
1. Supremacy and Authority
The AGM shall be the supreme decision -making organ of the Associ ation, and its
resolutions shall be binding on all other organs and members.
2. Convening and Notice
a) The AGM shall be convened once every academic year , preferably on the first
Saturday of March , by the Secretary -General under the direction of the Executiv e
Council.
b) A notice indicating the date, venue (or online platform), and agenda shall be
circulated to all registered members at least fourteen (14) days prior.
3. Agenda and Functions
The AGM shall:
a) Receive, consider, and adopt annual reports of the Ex ecutive Council, including
academic, welfare, and social activities.
b) Approve the audited financial report from the Finance Officer.
c) Receive the State of the Association Address from the outgoing President.
d) Conduct elections and inaugurate new offi ce bearers as provided under Chapter 5.
e) Deliberate on constitutional amendments
or policy resolutions.
f) Confer honorary or affiliate membership, where applicable.
g) Consider any other business duly communicated to the Secretary -General.
4. Quorum and Vo ting
a) The quorum shall be one-third (1/3) of registered active members.
b) Decisions shall be by simple majority , unless otherwise specified (e.g.,
constitutional amendments require two -thirds majority).
c) Voting may be by secret ballot, online, or hybr id, as determined by th e Executive
Council.
5. Special or Emergency Meetings
a) A Special General Meeting (EGM) may be convened by:
i. The Executive Council, by two-thirds (2/3) majority , or
ii. A written petition signed by one-quarter (1/4) of registered mem bers.
b) Notice shall be given at least seven (7) days in advance, and only the stated
business may be transacted.
6. Record Keeping
The Secretary -General shall ensure accurate minutes and resolutions are recorded,
signed, archived, and copies made available to members within fourteen (14) days .

AKUMS Constitution (Revised 2026 ) CHAPTER FIVE: ELECTIONS
Article 20: Authority of the Leadership Selection Committee
1. There shall be established a Leadership Selection Committee (LSC), which shall be
the sole body mandated to oversee, conduct, and con clude the selection of all leaders
of the Association through an interview -based process.
2. The LSC shall operate independently and shall not be subject to the direction, control,
or interference of the Executive Council or any other organ of the Association .
3. All leadership positions within the Association shall be filled exclusively through the
process conducted by the LSC, and no general elections shall be held.
Article 21: Composition of the Leadership Selection Committee
1. The Leadership Selection Committee shall consist of one (1) representative from each
academic year, from Year One (1) to Year Six (6).
2. Each academic year shall select its representative through a class -based democratic
process, the details of which shall be determined by the respective cla ss.
3. Members of the LSC shall, during their first sitting, elect from among themselves a
Chairperson and a Secretary.
4. A member of the LSC shall:
a) Be in good academic standing
b) Have no active disciplinary cases
c) Not be a candidate for any leadership po sition during that selection cycle
Article 22: Tenure and Vacancy
1. The Leadership Selection Committee shall be constituted at the beginning of each
leadership selection cycle and shall remain in office for the entire leadership term for
purposes of conducti ng the initial leadership selection and any special leadership
selection arising from a vacancy.
2. Upon the s
uccessful declaration and gazettement of leaders, the Committee shall
become dormant and shall be reconvened whenever a vacancy arises during the
leadership term.
3. The Leadership Selection Committee shall stand dissolved upon the expiry of the
leadership term and upon the completion of all special leadership selections, if any.
4. A member of the Leadership Selection Committee may be removed by their
respe ctive class through a two-thirds (⅔) class resolution for gross misconduct,
incapacity, or violation of this Constitution.
5. In the event of resignation, graduation, recall, or inability to serve, the concerned class
shall appoint a replacement within one (1) month , and such repl acement shall serve
for the remainder of the Committee’s term
Article 23: Independence and Accountability
1. The Leadership Selection Committee shall function independently of the Executive
Council and shall be accountable directly to the General Assembly.
2. The LSC shall be allocated an operational budget approved by the General Assembly
to facilitate the leadership selection process.

Article 24: Functions of the Leadership Selection Committee
1. The Leadership Selection Committee shall:
a) Declare all leadership p ositions vacant at the beginning of each leadership
cycle.
b) Determine and publish the leadership selection calendar.
c) Publicly call for applications and nominations for all leadership positions.
d) Receive, vet, and shortlist applicants based on merit, integrity, and
eligibility criteria.
e) Conduct structured interviews for all shortlisted candidates using a
standardized evaluation framework.
f) Deliberate and vote internally to select suitable candidates for each
leadership position.
g) Announce and g azette the selected leaders within seven (7) days of
conclusion of the interview process.
h) Serve as the advisory body on leadership selection, governance, and
constitutional interpretation relating to leadership transition.
Article 25: Eligibility to App ly for and Hold Office
1. General Eligibility
a) An applicant must be a duly registered and subscribed member of the Association.
b) An applicant must be in good academic standing as certified by the School of
Medicine.

matte r

Council

o The accused shall receive written notice of the allegations and a chance to
respond within seven (7) days.
o Hearings shall be conducted fairly, with both sides given a right to be heard
and present evidence or witnesses.
o Decisions shall be made by simple majority vote.

Depending on the severity of the offence, the Committee may recommend:
a) Written warning
b) Suspension from certain privileges or duties
